About the Maker

Hi, I'm Paul. Based right here in Chelmsley Wood, Birmingham, Pauly Bruv Engraving is born out of a lifelong passion for digital design and hands-on crafting.

Operating an Atomstack X7 diode laser, I specialize in transforming raw materials like natural slate, organic wood, and MDF into bespoke, precision-cut pieces. Every item is dialed in through LightBurn and crafted right here in the workshop.

When I'm not fulfilling custom orders, I'm usually spending time with my son, illustrating and writing children's books, or keeping right on with Birmingham City. Whether you need a personalized gift, a custom tactical coaster, or a layered street sign, every piece is made with proper Brummie pride.
